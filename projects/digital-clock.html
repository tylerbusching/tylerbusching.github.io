Overview

Designed and implemented a 24hr digital clock circuit using cascaded counters, decoders, switches, LEDs, and seven-segment displays.

What to Write Here

This circuit schematic displays a 24hr digital clock on seven segment displays which shows seconds, minutes, and hours. There is an LED Probe which indicates AM or PM hours. One of the biggest challenges when designing this schematic was solving how to cascade the counters in order to properly translate seconds over to minutes, and minutes over to hours. The logic gates at the bottom of the schematic allow for the clock to reset itself every 12 hours. In addition, there are two switches at the bottom so that the user can manually test the minute and hour block without having to wait in real-time.
